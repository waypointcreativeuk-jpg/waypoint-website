The Waypoint identity

A waypoint is a point on a route that shows you're heading the right way. Our identity is built on that idea: a pin that marks the destination, a route line that connects the steps, and a bold wordmark that is easy to recognise.

Electric blue on deep navy gives it energy without losing clarity, and a strict grid keeps everything balanced.

Questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Every project starts with a conversation, then a clear quote. Our first five clients receive a free starter project.

What is the free starter project?

A small piece of real work: a five-post social pack, a branded email template, or a one-page brand review. There's no cost and no obligation to work with us afterwards.

How long does a project take?

Starter projects are delivered within 7 days. Larger projects such as a full brand identity take longer, and we'll agree the timeline up front.

What do I receive at the end?

Finished files in the formats you need, ready to use, plus guidance on using them.
